My weekly usage

This target is for comparing your own usage. It never blocks the host administrator’s prompts; leave it blank to track this device’s estimated contribution without a target.

How your usage is estimated

Your meter shows the part of your assigned share attributed to this device. For example, using 50% of a 3% share means roughly 1.5 account-percentage points are attributed to this device; it does not mean the official account meter is 1.5%.

  • Input and output have different weights for each model. Output includes reasoning tokens reported by the provider.
  • Cached input receives a lower weight when the provider reports it. Fast has a higher estimated usage weight.
  • This is an approximation, not an exact OpenAI debit or charge. Reports can arrive late; unknown usage is not free usage.
  • Changing your share does not erase what you have used. Your weekly period follows the provider’s reset, not a separate local Monday reset.

Make your share last

  • Choose Standard when you do not need Fast.
  • Use a smaller model and lower thinking effort for simple tasks when suitable.
  • Stop responses you no longer need; stopping does not undo usage already recorded.
